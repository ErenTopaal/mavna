This is the widget from `@mavna/widget`, not a picture of it, and the quote it
renders comes off the live network: the chain list is the CCTP adapter's own
registry, the fee is Circle's, and the destination is inspected on Stellar
testnet before anything is priced.

Which is why there is a form first. A quote needs somewhere to send money to, and
a made-up one would turn every number below into decoration. Any Stellar address
works, including one that does not exist yet, in which case the quote will
include the reserve for creating it.

<Playground client:only="react" />

Nothing here can move money. There is no wallet, no signing and no submission on
this page, because the SDK does not do any of those things: it quotes and it
builds, and what you would sign belongs to your wallet.
