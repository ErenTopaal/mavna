## Daily caps

Two daily caps, counted per UTC day, bound the reserves the sponsor puts out:

- **Volume**: how many sponsorships can start in a day, set per source-chain class:
  `MAVNA_CAP_VOLUME_EXPENSIVE_PER_DAY` (300) for chains where gas is expensive and
  `MAVNA_CAP_VOLUME_CHEAP_PER_DAY` (200) for chains where gas is cheap.
- **Strandings**: `MAVNA_CAP_STRANDING_PER_DAY` (75). A stranding is a sponsorship whose repayment
  can never land: one the network refuses for good, or one whose account was set up and whose
  repayment's time limit passed unpaid. Past the cap, sponsorship pauses for the rest of the day.

The caps are checked when a client asks for an account setup, before anyone signs, and again when
the signed setup is submitted. A refusal answers `429` with a sentence that says which cap. When
the stored counts cannot be read, sponsorship pauses and the answer is `503`.

The source chain a request names is the caller's word, since the transfer has not started yet. So
every sponsorship counts against the smaller of the two volume caps, and in practice the daily
volume is the smaller setting. Raise that one to allow more.

The counts are stored under `MAVNA_STATE_DIR`, so a restart does not reset them.

## Channels

Each repayment is a transaction on a channel account of the sponsor's, one repayment per channel at
a time. The sponsor creates channels as it needs them, 2 XLM each, one at a time, and only when
every existing channel is busy.

- **A ten-minute window.** Asking for a repayment holds a channel for ten minutes from the first
  ask. Asking again or committing the signed repayment returns the same channel and window and
  never extends it.
- **Up to a day once set up.** When the account setup the repayment pays for lands, the channel is
  held until the repayment's own time limit, one day.
- **A lapsed window.** If no setup lands within the window, the channel goes back, the held
  repayment is dropped, and the account waits one more window before it can ask again.

Two limits bound the repayments not yet backed by a setup: `MAVNA_PROVISIONAL_SETTLEMENTS` (16)
across all clients and `MAVNA_SETTLEMENTS_PER_CLIENT` (2) per client address. Past either,
`/settlement` answers `503` or `429`.

The pool cannot run out within the caps. At most C sponsorships start in a UTC day, C being the
smaller volume cap, and each holds its channel for at most a day, so at most 2C are held at once.
With P unbacked repayments at most, the sponsor funds up to 2C + P channels: 416 at the defaults,
or 832 XLM if every one is ever created. `GET /health` reports this limit as `channels.maxSize`,
next to `size` and `leased`.

The sponsor funds at most 12 channels an hour; past that, `/settlement` answers `503` and the log
says channel funding is paused.

## The sweep

Every `MAVNA_SETTLE_SWEEP_SECONDS` (120), the sponsor runs a sweep that:

- submits held repayments whose money has arrived, so a closed page does not leave a reserve
  unpaid;
- delivers watched Circle transfers once Circle attests them (see
  [`POST /deliver`](/operate/api#post-deliver));
- tops a channel back up to 2 XLM once it falls below 1.5 XLM;
- merges a channel nobody has used for a day back into the sponsor, returning its XLM, while more
  than `MAVNA_CHANNEL_FLOOR` (4) channels remain;
- once an hour, compares the reserves the network says the sponsor carries with the ones its own
  records account for, and logs the numbers.

## Delivery budget

Each Circle delivery costs the sponsor a network fee whatever amount it carries. The sponsor pays
for none below `MAVNA_MIN_DELIVERY_USDC` (1 USDC) and spends at most `MAVNA_DELIVERY_BUDGET_XLM`
(25 XLM) on delivery fees in a UTC day. The spending is counted under `MAVNA_STATE_DIR`.
