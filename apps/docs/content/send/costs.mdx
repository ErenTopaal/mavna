---
title: What a transfer costs
description: Every cost line on a quote and the review, and what each one means.
---

The large amount on a route is what arrives on Stellar after its costs. Each cost is its own line
on the route or the review.

## Transfer fee

What the rail charges to carry the transfer, in the asset it is charged in, already taken out of the
amount that arrives. For Circle, the fee comes from Circle's fee service each time you get a quote.

## Network fees on the chain you send from

Your wallet pays the network fee for each transaction you approve, in that chain's own currency,
separately from the amount you send. LayerZero and Axelar also charge their delivery fee in the
sending chain's currency; it is paid with the send transaction, on top of the amount.

## Account reserve

Shown only when the address does not exist yet: 1.50 XLM for a new account that holds one asset.
The review calls it **Kept in your account**, because the XLM stays in the new account as its
reserve. The sponsor provides it up front, and part of the arriving money pays it back, capped at
an amount you approve before anything is sent. See
[Send to a new Stellar address](/send/new-address).

When the transfer also converts to another asset, the review adds
**Left unconverted to repay the reserve**: the part of the arrival kept back for that repayment.

On a deployment whose sponsor covers no reserves, an existing account pays its own reserve, shown
as **Reserve, from your own XLM**.

## Conversion

When you [receive XLM instead](/send/receive-xlm), the conversion runs at the exchange's price. It
has no fee line of its own; the route shows the **Conversion rate** and the minimum you receive.

## Integrator fee

A product that embeds Mavna can add its own fee on routes that convert on arrival. When it does,
the review shows **Integrator fee** with the amount and who receives it. The Mavna app sets no
integrator fee.
