---
title: Receive XLM instead
description: Send USDC and have the recipient end up holding XLM, converted on a Stellar exchange in the same transfer.
---

XLM is Stellar's own currency. The money crosses as USDC and is converted to XLM on a Stellar
exchange as the last step of the same transfer.

## Switch to XLM

Under **Receive on Stellar**, switch from `USDC` to `XLM`. The routes update and show:

- the amount of XLM you receive, and under it the **Min**: the least XLM the conversion delivers;
- **Conversion rate**, the XLM that one USDC buys at the quoted price;
- **Converted on**, the Stellar exchange that quoted the conversion.

The rate is the market's. The conversion has no fee line of its own.

## Approve the conversion

Because the conversion spends from the receiving account, the recipient approves it in a Stellar
wallet connected to that account. The step reads **Convert to XLM on Stellar** and comes after the
USDC arrives. [Connect your wallets](/send/wallets) covers connecting one.

The conversion spends only what this transfer brought in. Anything the account already held is
left alone.

## If the price moves

Before you approve, the app asks the exchange for a fresh price. If converting now would deliver
less XLM than the minimum you were shown, the app stops before asking for your signature and the
USDC stays in the account. It is yours either way, and you can convert it later.
