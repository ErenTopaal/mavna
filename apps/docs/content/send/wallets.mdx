---
title: Connect your wallets
description: Which wallets the app connects on each side of a transfer, and when you need a Stellar wallet.
---

A transfer has two sides: the chain your money is on, and Stellar. You always connect a wallet on
the sending side. You connect a Stellar wallet only when the transfer needs the receiving account to
approve something.

Press **Connect** in the top bar of the app. The menu has two sections, **Source wallet** and
**Stellar wallet**, and each has its own **Disconnect**.

## Source wallet

The menu lists every wallet your browser offers to the page, by name. Choose yours and approve the
connection in it. If no wallet is installed, the menu says **No wallet found in this browser**.

This is the wallet that holds your USDC or USDT0 and approves the sending side of the transfer. When
it is on a different network from the chain you send from, the app asks it to switch.

## Stellar wallet

Press **Connect** under **Stellar wallet** and choose one of Freighter, xBull, Albedo, Lobstr, Hana
or Rabet. The wallet has to hold the account the transfer goes to: if you connect a different
account, the app says which one it needs.

You need a Stellar wallet in these cases:

- **Sending to a new address**, where the account approves the repayment and its own setup. See
  [Send to a new Stellar address](/send/new-address).
- **Receiving another asset than the one that crosses**, such as XLM, where the account approves the
  conversion. See [Receive XLM instead](/send/receive-xlm).
- **A deployment whose sponsor covers no costs**, where the account signs the delivery on Stellar
  and pays its network fee in XLM.

For a USDC transfer to an account that can already hold USDC, with the sponsor paying for the
delivery, you need no Stellar wallet at all.
