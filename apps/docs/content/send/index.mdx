Mavna moves USDC and USDT0 from other chains to any Stellar address. You see what arrives
before you sign anything, and nothing leaves your wallet until you approve it there.

## What you need

- A browser wallet that holds USDC or USDT0 on one of the EVM chains the app lists
  under **Send**. [Connect your wallets](/send/wallets) explains which wallets the app
  finds.
- The Stellar address the money goes to. It starts with `G` and is 56 characters long. It can be an
  address that does not exist on Stellar yet.

## Send your first transfer

1. Open the Mavna app at [mavna.app](https://mavna.app). The **Send to Stellar** card opens.
2. Press the chain next to **Send** and pick the chain your money is on.
3. Type the amount.
4. Press **Recipient**, paste the Stellar address, and press **Use this address**. Routes appear as
   each rail answers, with the amount that arrives on each.
5. Press **Connect a wallet**, then press **Review transfer**, check the summary, and press
   **Start transfer**.
6. Approve each prompt in your wallet. The card shows each step as it happens and ends with
   **Arrived on Stellar**.

The full walk through every control and prompt is in [Send USDC to Stellar](/send/send-usdc).
