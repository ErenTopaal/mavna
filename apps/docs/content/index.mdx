# Mavna

Mavna moves USDC and USDT0 into Stellar. It asks every rail that can carry a transfer at
once, shows what actually arrives after fees and who you are trusting to deliver
it, and completes the transfer end to end, including to a Stellar address that
does not exist yet and holds no XLM.

A mavna is the lighter: the flat-bottomed barge that carries cargo between a ship
and the shore when the vessel cannot dock itself. The name is the delivery, not
the crossing.

## Three ways in

**[Use the app](/guides).** Open [mavna.app](https://mavna.app), pick a chain,
paste a Stellar address, and see what arrives before you send anything. No
account, no email, and nothing to install beyond the wallets you already have.

**[Build with the SDK](/developers).** npm packages that put every rail behind
one interface, rank routes, price the conversion into the asset you want, and
hand back unsigned transactions for your own signing flow. The SDK never signs,
never submits, and never holds a key.

**[Embed the widget](/widget).** One React component that renders the quote, the
confirmation, and the wait, and connects the wallets itself. It is styled by CSS
custom properties, so it looks like your product.

## What it moves, and from where

Four rails, each a separate adapter behind one interface, each with its own
trust model. The engine races every rail on offer on each quote and shows the
differences side by side.

| Rail | Trust model | Source chains | Assets into Stellar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Circle CCTP | The issuer burns and mints; nobody holds the money in between | 26 on testnet, 25 on mainnet: EVM, Solana, Starknet | USDC |
| Axelar ITS | A named validator set attests | Resolved at runtime from Axelar's registry | Interchain Token Service tokens, USDC among them on mainnet |
| LayerZero OFT | Verifiers chosen by whoever deployed the token | 12 on mainnet, 3 on testnet | USDT0 on mainnet, the only route to native USDT on Stellar; any OFT you deploy yourself |
| NEAR Intents | Competing solvers deliver from their own inventory | 13 EVM chains, mainnet only | USDC and XLM |

On top of the rails, a swap leg converts what a bridge delivers into the asset
you asked for, priced on Stellar's own exchange. That is how "send USDC, receive
XLM" is one flow rather than a bridge followed by a manual trade.

The network is a single configuration value. The deployment you use today runs on
Stellar's test network, and the same code runs on mainnet: what differs between
them is which of each rail's routes its own deployment carries.
[Rails and networks](/developers/rails) states that difference precisely.

## The part no other Stellar product does

A Stellar account cannot hold USDC without a trustline, and a trustline costs a
reserve in XLM that someone arriving from another chain does not have. Mavna
completes transfers to a brand-new Stellar address holding zero XLM: a sponsor
fronts the reserve, the account is created and made able to receive inside the
flow, and the deposit repays itself out of the arriving funds. You are never
asked to buy XLM first, and the word "trustline" never appears as a task.
[How sponsorship works](/developers/concepts/sponsorship) explains the mechanism;
[as a user you just paste an address](/guides/new-account).
