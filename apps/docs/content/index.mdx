---
title: Mavna documentation
description: Guides and reference for the Mavna app, the widget, the SDK and the sponsor service.
---

Mavna moves USDC and USDT0 from other chains to Stellar. It asks every rail that can carry a
transfer at the same time, shows what arrives on each and who you trust to deliver it, and
completes the transfer, including to a Stellar address that does not exist yet.

It comes in three forms that share one engine: the Mavna app, a widget you embed in a React app,
and an SDK of packages you build on. The SDK builds transactions and never signs them: every
transaction that moves the user's money is signed in the user's own wallet.

<Paths />

Mavna is open source under the Apache-2.0 license. The source is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/ErenTopaal/mavna).
