# Concepts

Short pages, each one idea, in the order they matter for building a real flow.

- [Assets and amounts](/developers/concepts/model): identity is a chain plus an
  identifier, and money is an integer string.
- [Routes and rejections](/developers/concepts/routes): race every rail at once,
  render answers as they land, and treat a refusal as data.
- [Trust models](/developers/concepts/trust): the comparison the routing engine
  exists to show.
- [Signing](/developers/concepts/signing): how many signatures a route asks for,
  which approvals are skippable, and the Stellar-side signatures the sponsor flow
  collects.
- [Sponsorship](/developers/concepts/sponsorship): how a Stellar address with
  zero XLM receives funds.
- [Delivery](/developers/concepts/delivery): attestation, the mint, proving
  arrival, and why retrying is safe.
- [Status and recovery](/developers/concepts/status): lookup keys, resumption,
  and telling "slow" from "stalled".
- [Arriving as another asset](/developers/concepts/swap): the swap leg on
  Stellar's exchanges, and conversion on arrival.
