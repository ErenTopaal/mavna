# Send USDT0 to Stellar

If your dollars are USDT rather than USDC, send USDT0. It travels over LayerZero,
the only route to native USDT on Stellar, and arrives as USDT0 on Stellar.

## Pick USDT0

On the **Send to Stellar** screen, switch the asset beside the amount from `USDC` to
`USDT0`. Two things follow from that switch:

- **Send from** now lists the chains USDT0 can leave from: Ethereum, Arbitrum,
  Optimism, Polygon, Ink, Unichain, Sei, Hyperliquid EVM, X Layer, Berachain, Flare
  and Rootstock. A chain stays in the list even when its USDT0 deployment cannot reach
  Stellar at the moment, and the quote then says why.
- **Receive on Stellar** shows `USDT0` and nothing else. USDT0 is not converted on
  arrival, so the recipient holds USDT0.

## What you send and what arrives

The token you send is the one the chain's USDT0 contract moves, read from that
contract when you quote, never from a list of names. What arrives is the Stellar
asset the USDT0 contract on Stellar credits, read from that contract's own storage:
`USDT0` issued by `GATISXX6BZ6NC7IKQBY37CJD4SOZL3CYZJWXEDG6JVIY4WBS6KXJHN6Q`. Anyone
can issue an asset called USDT0, so check the issuer if you add it to a wallet by
hand.

## The receiving account

The recipient needs a Stellar account that already holds a USDT0 trustline. If it
does not, the screen tells you before anything is signed.

A brand-new account cannot receive USDT0 through Mavna yet. Setting one up is paid
back in USDC from what arrives, and USDC is the only asset the sponsor curates, so a
USDT0 route to a new address is declined at quote time with that reason. Send to an
account that exists and can hold USDT0, or [send USDC](/guides/send) instead.

## Costs and time

LayerZero's fee is paid in the source chain's own gas token, separately from the
amount, and the quote shows it in that token. The least you receive is enforced by
the USDT0 contract itself, from the slippage you chose. How long delivery takes
depends on the confirmations USDT0 asks for on the chain you send from, so the same
amount can take seconds from Arbitrum and many minutes from Ethereum. The quote works
the estimate out per chain.

## Test network

USDT0 exists on the main network only. On the test network the asset can be picked,
and the quote explains that this route carries no token there.

## Find it later

A USDT0 transfer shows in **Activity** like any other, and can be
[found again](/guides/track) from its source transaction alone. Its status is read
from LayerZero's own message index.
