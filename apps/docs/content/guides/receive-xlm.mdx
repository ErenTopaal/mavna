# Arrive as XLM

You can send USDC and have the recipient arrive holding XLM, Stellar's own currency,
instead of USDC. It is one toggle on the **Send to Stellar** screen and one flow,
not a bridge followed by a manual trade.

## Switch the receive asset

Under **Receive on Stellar**, switch from `USDC` to `XLM`. The quote updates: it now
shows the XLM amount you receive and the least you can receive, because the
conversion runs at the live market rate and the rate can move between the quote and
the moment it executes.

## What happens

The transfer bridges USDC exactly as before, and then a conversion step runs on
Stellar's own exchange, turning the arrived USDC into XLM. The conversion spends from
the receiving account, so the recipient signs it in their Stellar wallet; a
[Stellar wallet](/guides/wallets) is needed on the receiving side for this reason.

The rate is the market's. There is no separate conversion fee line: the quote shows
the rate's outcome and the floor you agreed to. If the market has moved past that
floor when the conversion runs, it refuses whole rather than deliver less, and the
recipient keeps the USDC that arrived. The USDC is theirs either way.

[How the swap leg works](/developers/concepts/swap) covers the mechanism.
