# Your first transfer

You have USDC on another chain and you want it on Stellar. This takes one page, two
wallets, and a few minutes. You see exactly what arrives before you send anything,
and nothing moves until you confirm it in your own wallet.

Open [mavna.app](https://mavna.app).

## 1. Say what you are sending

On **Send to Stellar**, set four things:

- **Send from**: the chain your USDC is on. The list is every chain Mavna can move
  from on the current network.
- **The amount**, in USDC. Routes update as you type; there is no button to press
  to see a quote.
- **Receive on Stellar**: leave it on `USDC` to arrive holding USDC, or switch to
  `XLM` to arrive holding Stellar's own currency. [Arriving as
  XLM](/guides/receive-xlm) is its own short guide.
- **Recipient**: the Stellar address it lands on. Any address works, including
  [one that does not exist yet](/guides/new-account).

## 2. Read the quote

Every rail that can carry your transfer answers, and the routes appear as the
answers land, ranked so the first one is the best net outcome. Each route shows the
amount that actually arrives after every fee, how long it takes, how many approvals
your wallets will be asked for, and [who you are
trusting](/developers/concepts/trust) to deliver it. You can open a route for the
full breakdown, or pick a different one.

A rail that cannot carry your transfer says why in a sentence rather than silently
not appearing. On the test network most rails usually decline, because
their own test deployments carry almost nothing; [rails and
networks](/developers/rails) has the background.

## 3. Connect and send

Connect the wallet that holds your USDC. Any browser wallet that announces itself
works; [wallets that work](/guides/wallets) has the list for both sides. When
everything is ready, press **Review transfer**, check the summary, and press
**Start transfer**.

From there, everything that happens is a prompt in one of your wallets, and each
prompt says in plain words what it does. For a destination that already holds USDC,
there are two: one that allows Circle to move your USDC, once, for this exact
amount, and one that sends it. Sending to [a brand-new
address](/guides/new-account) adds a Stellar-side signature or two, and the page
walks you through them.

## 4. Wait, briefly

The page narrates the wait: your transfer is sent, Circle attests it, and Mavna
delivers it on Stellar. Attestation takes a few minutes, and the page says so while
it happens: it is safe to close the tab. Every completed step links to the block
explorer for the chain it happened on.

When it lands, the page says **Arrived on Stellar**, and the funds are in the
destination account.

## If you close the tab

Nothing is lost. Any transfer can be found again from the transaction you sent or
the address you sent to, on any device, with nothing saved: [find a
transfer](/guides/track). If a delivery is taking long, you can [finish it
yourself](/guides/finish), and retrying is always safe.
