# Find a transfer

A transfer is not tied to the browser you started it in. You can find it again from
the transaction you sent or the address you sent to, on any device, with nothing
saved anywhere. Watching a transfer needs no wallet.

## Look it up

Open **Activity** and paste one of two things:

- **The transaction you sent** from the source chain, or
- **the Stellar address you sent to**.

Press **Find**. Mavna reads the chain and tells you where the transfer is:
delivered, on its way, returned, or nothing found at this address yet. If it is a
transaction or address it does not recognise, it says so rather than guessing.

Either key is enough on its own. Being able to find a transfer from the destination
address is the part most bridges cannot do, because their records are keyed on the
sending transaction.

## This browser remembers too

Under **From this browser**, Activity lists the transfers you started here, each with
a **Check** to refresh its status and a link to the source transaction. This list is
a convenience, not the record: it can be cleared, and every transfer in it is still
findable by the lookup above. If a transfer is taking long, you can [finish the
delivery yourself](/guides/finish).
