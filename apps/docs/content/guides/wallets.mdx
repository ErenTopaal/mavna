# Wallets

A transfer has two sides: the chain your USDC is on, and Stellar. You always connect
a wallet on the sending side, and you connect one on the Stellar side when the
transfer needs a signature there.

## The sending side

Under **Source wallet**, Mavna lists every browser wallet that announces itself, by
name. Any standard browser wallet works; there is no allowlist and nothing to
install beyond the wallet you already have. If your wallet is on a different network
from the chain you are sending from, you are asked to switch, and declining leaves
everything unsent.

This is the wallet that holds your USDC and signs the approval and the send.

## The Stellar side

Under **Stellar wallet**, Mavna connects through the Stellar Wallets Kit, which
offers Freighter, xBull, Albedo, Lobstr, Hana, and Rabet.

You need a Stellar wallet on the receiving side in two cases:

- **Sending to a brand-new address**, where the receiving wallet signs to set the
  account up and to repay the reserve. [Send to a new address](/guides/new-account)
  walks through it.
- **Receiving as XLM**, where the receiving wallet signs the conversion, because it
  spends from the receiving account. [Arrive as XLM](/guides/receive-xlm) covers it.

Sending USDC to an address that already holds USDC needs no Stellar wallet: the
sending wallet does all the signing, and Mavna delivers the funds. That is how
mavna.app works today, with Mavna's sponsor paying for the delivery on Stellar.

A Mavna deployment can also run with the sponsor paying for nothing. There, the
receiving wallet connects and signs Circle's delivery on Stellar, paying its small
fee from its own XLM, and opens its own trustline when it needs one; an address
that does not exist yet cannot be sent to. The page says which applies before
you send.
