# Send USDC to Stellar

This goes through the **Send to Stellar** screen at
[mavna.app](https://mavna.app) control by control. For the short version, start
with [your first transfer](/guides).

## The form

**Send from** is the chain your USDC is on. Opening it lists the chains you can
send from, split into the ones you can sign from in this browser and the ones that
quote but need a wallet for another chain family, which the list labels rather than
letting you find out after choosing.

**The amount** is in USDC, and typing re-quotes on its own; there is no button to
ask for a quote. The switch beside it changes what you send to USDT0, which
[Send USDT0](/guides/send-usdt0) covers. When your wallet is connected, the screen shows your balance and a
**Max** control that fills in the full amount.

**Receive on Stellar** decides what you end up holding: `USDC`, or `XLM` through a
conversion on a Stellar exchange (Soroswap, SushiSwap or Aquarius). With `XLM` selected, the quote shows the XLM
amount you receive; [arrive as XLM](/guides/receive-xlm) covers the differences.

**Recipient** takes the destination. Paste a `G...` address. If it is not a valid
Stellar address the screen tells you so; an address that cannot receive is refused
at quote time, before anything is signed.

Two rows below summarise the transfer before you commit: **Route priority**, which
you can switch between best return and fastest, and **You will approve**, which
counts the signatures the selected route will ask for.

## The quote

Routes appear as each rail answers, ranked so the first is the best net outcome, and
the best-returning and fastest routes are badged. Each route shows what arrives
after every fee, an honest time estimate, how many approvals your wallets will ask
for, and the trust model in plain words. Open one for the full breakdown: the rail,
the transfer fee, the minimum you receive, the account reserve when there is one,
and where it settles.

A rail that declines shows up with its reason when you turn on "show rails that
declined". On the test network most of them usually decline, because their own
test deployments carry almost nothing, and the reason on screen says exactly that.

## Sending

Connect the wallet that holds your USDC, press **Review transfer**, and press
**Start transfer**. What follows is a sequence of wallet prompts, each described in
words as it runs:

1. **Allow Circle to move your USDC.** A one-time approval for exactly the amount
   you are sending. If you have already approved this amount, the step is skipped.
2. **Send from your chain.** The transaction that moves the money.

If you decline a prompt, the page says plainly that nothing was sent. If your
balance cannot cover the amount, you are told before anything is signed, and if
your wallet is on a different network you are asked to switch.

For a destination that is a brand-new Stellar account, the receiving wallet signs
too. [Send to a new address](/guides/new-account) walks through those prompts.

## While it moves

The page narrates the wait step by step: sent, then Circle attests the transfer,
then Mavna delivers it on Stellar. (On a deployment where the sponsor funds nothing,
that last step is a prompt in the receiving wallet, which pays the delivery's small
XLM fee. mavna.app's sponsor delivers it for you.) Attestation takes a few minutes, and the page
says it is safe to close the page while it happens. Every on-chain step links to the
block explorer for the chain it happened on.

If the wait outlives the estimate, nothing is wrong with your funds. The delivery
can be triggered again safely at any time, from this page or later from
[Activity](/guides/finish), and a repeat delivery cannot happen twice.

## After it lands

**Arrived on Stellar** means the funds are in the destination account. If you chose
to receive XLM, one more wallet prompt converts the arrived USDC at a freshly quoted
rate, and if the market has moved past the floor you agreed to, the conversion
refuses whole and you keep the USDC.

The transfer is remembered in this browser under **Activity**, and it is always
recoverable without that memory: [find a transfer](/guides/track).
