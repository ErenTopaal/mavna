# Finish a delivery

Most transfers deliver on their own. Once the page has said it is safe to close,
the sponsor keeps checking with Circle and delivers the transfer without you, for up
to two days. If the page said closing would pause it, or a delivery is taking longer
than usual, you can trigger it yourself. Retrying is
always safe: a delivery cannot happen twice.

## Finish it

Open **Activity** and [find the transfer](/guides/track) from the transaction you
sent. When a transfer can be finished, a **Finish the delivery** button appears.
Finishing needs the source transaction, so paste that rather than the destination
address if you have the choice.

Press it, and one of a few things happens:

- **Delivered.** The funds are on Stellar, and the status updates to say so.
- **Not attested yet.** Circle has not finished attesting the transfer. Nothing is
  wrong; try again in a minute.
- **Part of it arrived.** The status shows how much, and finishing the rest is safe
  to try again.

## Who pays for finishing

On mavna.app, the sponsor pays for the delivery on Stellar. On a deployment where
the sponsor funds nothing, the receiving wallet connects, signs the delivery and
pays its small fee from its own XLM (up to 0.1 XLM).

## A conversion on arrival that has not run

A transfer that converts on arrival lands at the Mavna Arrival Contract, and
LayerZero's executor runs the conversion. If it has not, the transfer shows as
delivered with the conversion pending, and it can be finished by anyone: the
contract runs only the exact message that is waiting, with your minimum enforced
inside it. With the sponsor funding, the sponsor finishes overdue ones; otherwise
your Stellar wallet can, and the review showed **If you finish it yourself** with
what that costs. If the conversion cannot meet your minimum, the token you sent
arrives as it came.

## Why it is safe

A delivery is idempotent: the network records that a transfer has been delivered, so
triggering it a second time cannot mint the funds twice. If a transfer's money has
not been delivered, finishing delivers it; if it already has, finishing does
nothing. There is no state in which pressing the button costs you. [How delivery is
proven](/developers/concepts/delivery) covers why.
