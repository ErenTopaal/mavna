# Finish a delivery

Most transfers deliver on their own. If one is taking longer than usual, or you
closed the tab during the wait, you can trigger the delivery yourself. Retrying is
always safe: a delivery cannot happen twice.

## Finish it

Open **Activity** and [find the transfer](/guides/track) from the transaction you
sent. When a transfer can be finished, a **Finish the delivery** button appears.
Finishing needs the source transaction, so paste that rather than the destination
address if you have the choice.

Press it, and one of a few things happens:

- **Delivered.** The funds are on Stellar, and the status updates to say so.
- **Not attested yet.** Circle has not finished attesting the transfer. Nothing is
  wrong; try again in a minute.
- **Part of it arrived.** The status shows how much, and finishing the rest is safe
  to try again.

## Why it is safe

A delivery is idempotent: the network records that a transfer has been delivered, so
triggering it a second time cannot mint the funds twice. If a transfer's money has
not been delivered, finishing delivers it; if it already has, finishing does
nothing. There is no state in which pressing the button costs you. [How delivery is
proven](/developers/concepts/delivery) covers why.
