# What a transfer costs

Every quote shows what actually arrives after all costs, and the headline number is
that arriving amount. Nothing is deducted silently. These are the lines you can
meet.

## Transfer fee

What the rail charges for the crossing, already deducted from the amount shown as
arriving. On Circle's standard path into Stellar this is zero at the time of
writing: the quote reads Circle's live rate each time, so the number on screen is
the current one, not a promise from this page.

## Gas on the chain you send from

Your wallet pays the network fee for its own transactions, in that chain's own
currency. Your wallet shows this before you confirm, and Mavna adds nothing to it.

## Account reserve, only for a brand-new address

Sending to a Stellar address that does not exist yet shows one line in XLM, labelled
**Kept in your account**: 1.5 XLM for a new account able to hold one asset. This is
not a fee anyone keeps. A sponsor fronts it so you never need XLM to receive, a
small slice of the arriving funds buys it back at the live rate up to a cap you
agree to, and the XLM ends up locked in the new account as its own reserve, returned
to you if the account is ever closed. [Send to a new
address](/guides/new-account) and [how sponsorship
works](/developers/concepts/sponsorship) explain it.

## Conversion, only when you receive XLM

Choosing to arrive as XLM converts the arrived USDC on Stellar's own exchange at the
live rate. There is no separate conversion fee line: the rate is the market's, the
quote shows the least you can receive, and the conversion refuses whole rather than
deliver below that floor.

## What Mavna charges

Nothing. There is no Mavna fee on any route in the app. Products that
[embed the widget](/widget) can add their own fee, and when they do it appears as
its own line naming who receives it.
