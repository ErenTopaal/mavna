destination is checked on Stellar. Enter an amount and a Stellar address and it

## The full configurator

[playground.mavna.app](https://playground.mavna.app) is the widget with every knob
exposed. A control rail writes a config, the widget on stage renders with it, and a
dock beneath prints the exact config an integrator would paste and logs the events
the widget emits. Change the variant, the rails, the priority, the hidden UI, and
the signal colour, and watch the component and its config change together.

It covers the two embeddable layouts, the inline card and the drawer. The wide
layout is the full app, which lives at [mavna.app](https://mavna.app).

Every field it writes is on the [configuration](/widget/configuration) page, and
every colour it sets is a [theme](/widget/theming) token.
