[playground.mavna.app](https://playground.mavna.app) is the widget with its options exposed. A panel
on the left writes a config, the widget renders with it, and a panel underneath prints the config to
paste into your app and logs the events the widget sends.

The panel covers:

- **Layout**: the compact or drawer variant, and the appearance.
- **Routing**: the route priority and the rails.
- **Hide UI**: each control `hiddenUI` can remove.
- **Recipient**: a prefilled address, and whether it is locked.
- **Form start**: the starting chain and amount.
- **Theme**: ready-made looks (Default, Midnight, Ocean, Forest, Plum, Rose and Sand), a tint colour
  and strength for the grounds and surfaces in light and dark, corners, the text font, whether
  figures follow it, and the signal colour. The config tab shows the theme they produce.

The full-page `wide` layout is the Mavna app itself, at [mavna.app](https://mavna.app).

Every field the playground writes is described in [Configuration](/widget/configuration).
