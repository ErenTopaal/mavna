`variant` chooses how the widget sits in your page. The flow inside is the same in all three.

## Compact

`variant: "compact"` is the default: one card that holds the form, the routes and the transfer. It
takes the width of its container, so it fits in a sidebar or a checkout panel. The widget on
[Embed the widget](/widget) is this layout.

## Wide

`variant: "wide"` is the full-page layout the Mavna app uses: a header with the network toggle,
the wallet button and the menu, and the form with the routes beside it. Use it when the widget is
the page.

## Drawer

`variant: "drawer"` puts the compact card in a popup that rises from the bottom over a dimmed page.
By default it comes with its own button, labelled **Bring funds to Stellar**. It closes on its close
button, the dimmed backdrop, or the Escape key. Escape first closes a menu open inside the widget,
or goes back from a screen such as the chain picker, and closes the drawer only when there is
neither. While your user's wallet is showing a prompt, the drawer stays open.

<LiveWidget variant="drawer" />

To open it from your own button instead, pass the `open` prop: the widget then shows no button of
its own, and `onClose` is called once the closing animation has finished.

```tsx
const [open, setOpen] = useState(false);

<MavnaWidget config={config} open={open} onClose={() => setOpen(false)} />;
```

Or take a ref and call its methods:

```tsx
const ref = useRef<MavnaWidgetRef>(null);

<MavnaWidget ref={ref} config={config} />;

// ref.current?.openDrawer(), closeDrawer(), toggleDrawer(), isOpen()
```
